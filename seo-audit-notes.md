# SEO master cleanup

This file records the SEO implementation priorities for pericsistemi.rs.

## Technical
- Canonicals must use https://pericsistemi.rs/ exclusively.
- Sitemap and image sitemap must use the custom domain.
- Keep robots.txt pointing to both sitemaps.
- Add favicon and descriptive image alt text.
- Normalize title tags and meta descriptions.
- Use BreadcrumbList, Service, Article and Organization/WebSite structured data only where semantically appropriate.

## Content
- Expand thin commercial pages with original, technically accurate content.
- Prioritize installation, sliding gates, swing gates, service, preparation, photocells, pricing and brand pages.
- Do not publish doorway pages or city pages with substantially duplicated text.
- Create project/case-study pages only from verified installations and real photographs.

## Authority
- Keep Google Business Profile data consistent with the real business details.
- Build genuine reviews, photographs, citations and relevant links.
- Measure queries, impressions, CTR, average position and leads in Search Console.

## Technical accuracy
- Product specifications must be checked against the manufacturer's current documentation before publication.
- Never invent model capacities, warranties, standards or compatibility claims.
